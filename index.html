About Kalma

Slow down. Stay a while.

Kalma is how we say calm. We built this space for the hours after work and after dinner, when you want somewhere warm to sit, talk and not be rushed.

Come for a Spanish Latte, stay for a round of board games, and leave when you're ready. We're open late into the night, every day except Tuesday.
